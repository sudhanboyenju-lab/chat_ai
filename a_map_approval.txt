Map Approval Service (घर नक्सा पास)

To get building map approval, the following documents are required:
1. Application form in prescribed format
2. Land ownership certificate (Lalpurja) - original and copy
3. Citizenship certificate copy of landowner
4. Certified survey map (trace map) from survey office
5. Four-boundary certificate from ward office
6. Building design map prepared by licensed engineer or architect
7. Property tax payment receipt for current fiscal year

Process:
1. Submit application with documents to Ward Office for recommendation
2. Ward technical staff conducts site inspection
3. Register map at Municipality's Urban Development/Technical branch
4. Pay prescribed fee, receive temporary permit for construction
5. After construction completion, receive permanent map approval certificate

Fees and rules may vary by municipality - contact your local ward office for details.
